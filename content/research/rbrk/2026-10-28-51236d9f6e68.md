## Investment thesis

Rubrik sells cyber recovery and data-security subscriptions to organizations that need to restore essential systems after an attack. Recurring subscriptions and expansion into identity and AI-agent security could turn a large installed base into durable cash generation. The investment depends on sustained recurring-revenue growth, improving cash conversion and dilution that does not absorb that growth. At $136.00 on October 28, the security assessment is TRIM because the price exceeds the retained $121.72 probability-weighted valuation and offers negative remaining modeled reward/risk. The model dates to October 13 and remains conditional on the August quarterly evidence. The strongest contrary case is that new security products expand the addressable spending opportunity enough to sustain the high-growth bull path. Product announcements support that possibility only after they turn into recurring revenue and cash.

## How the business creates value

Customers pay to protect, govern and recover data across enterprise and cloud environments. Cyber recovery is essential after a breach, and established integrations can encourage renewals and expansion. New AI-agent security products extend the opportunity but also add competition and execution demands. Subscription annual recurring revenue is a contracted run-rate measure, not the same thing as recognized sales, cash collection or net earnings.

The August 27 release for the July quarter reported $427.3 million revenue versus $309.9 million a year earlier, subscription ARR of $1.66 billion, operating cash flow of $76.8 million and free cash flow of $65.7 million. Cash and short-term investments were $1.75 billion. GAAP loss per unit was $0.30 despite adjusted profit of $0.20. Positive cash generation supports survivability, but the GAAP pre-profit flag remains appropriate; compensation and convertible dilution matter to eventual per-unit earnings. [Primary evidence](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1943896/000194389626000055/rubrikinc-991pressrelease7.htm).

The retained October 13 base case assumes $4.6 billion FY2030 sales, 24% normalized net margin and 275 million diluted units. That requires substantial compounding from the recent quarter and eventual conversion from GAAP losses to normalized earnings. The 55 x bull earnings multiple and 30% bull margin describe optimistic economics; they are analyst estimates, not an issuer promise. The model is carried forward with its date; this price review does not advance its financial vintage.

## What could break the thesis

Security products may grow quickly while sales expense, cloud delivery costs and compensation keep owner earnings below the normalized margin case. Larger vendors could bundle recovery/security tools and erode pricing or retention. The warning signs are a sustained slowdown in net-new ARR, weaker cash conversion, increasing dilution and missed delivery of recovery functionality. The strongest upside challenge is successful cross-selling of agent-security products without a matching increase in acquisition cost.

Thesis **INTACT**; credibility **PASS**; GAAP pre-profit **Yes**; binary-event risk **MEDIUM**. The 25% plausible overnight gap is an analyst stress, not a forecast bound. A daily-close stop cannot guarantee execution at its level. For HIGH binary risk, the larger of stop distance and gap stress governs sizing.

**Evidence gap:** Complete current gap distributions, material capital claims and synchronized insider/short evidence are unavailable. Test the specific business failures above and refresh immediately on results, financing, governance or thesis-changing news.
