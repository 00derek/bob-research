## Investment thesis

Sandisk sells NAND storage used to keep data accessible when AI workloads exceed expensive fast-memory capacity. At the **$835.06 November 6 completed close**, the November 7 re-underwritten model gives **$1,542.21 probability-weighted present value (+84.7%)**. The security assessment is **WATCH**. The current security does not yet satisfy the full starter valuation, reward/risk and predeclared-close tests. The favorable case is durable enterprise demand and customer commitments supporting normalized returns. The opposing case is rapid NAND price compression just as new supply arrives, leaving still-large venture obligations and severe permanent downside.

**Evidence gap:** Future margins, capital needs and terminal multiples remain analyst estimates. The body distinguishes released financials, issuer plans and modeled economics; missing current consensus and event evidence prevent treating the estimate as a forecast guarantee.

## How the business creates value

Datacenter SSDs combine NAND capacity, controllers and firmware into qualified systems. Training and inference retain checkpoints, retrieval data and increasingly context; this increases bytes stored without guaranteeing revenue per bit. Sandisk shares wafer manufacturing with Kioxia, so bit supply, process transitions and partner funding matter as much as end demand. Bi CS upgrades can lower cost per bit while enterprise qualification supports differentiation. Samsung, SK hynix and Micron remain capable suppliers; higher industry capacity can eventually transfer value from producers to buyers. The new model does not assign a separate speculative premium to high-bandwidth flash.

Micron reported fiscal Q4 2026 core data-center revenue of $18.002 billion versus $11.524 billion sequentially, with 90% gross margin. That segment includes products beyond NAND, so it does not establish Sandisk market share; it does corroborate strong supplier economics across the memory cycle. Its investment in more capacity is also a reason not to capitalize peak NAND profits indefinitely. [Micron September 30 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000018/a2026q4ex991-pressrelease.htm). **Evidence gap:** A synchronized NAND-only supply, cost and market-share panel was not reconstructed. Product-level customer pricing and competitive qualification remain undisclosed; those gaps limit industry confidence despite reported demand.
