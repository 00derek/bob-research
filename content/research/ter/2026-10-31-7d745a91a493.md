## Investment thesis

Teradyne sells equipment that verifies complex chips and systems before they reach customers. More AI devices, high-bandwidth memory and high-speed links increase the test workload, creating an opportunity beyond ordinary semiconductor unit growth. The October 27 quarter demonstrates that demand, but also a sequential decline from a very strong Q2 and a lower fourth-quarter profit guide. This new October 31 model uses the latest cash, minority claims and investment commitment rather than annualizing peak adjusted EPS. WATCH follows a weighted value near but below the October 30 price, without the upside required for a new entry. The favorable case is durable test intensity and share gains across compute and memory; the opposing case is that customer capex timing and a concentrated equipment cycle pull margins back before the forecast is earned.

## How the business creates value

Chipmakers and outsourced test houses buy systems to detect defects, meet performance specifications and improve usable yield. Higher complexity, advanced packaging and HBM can increase test time per device; better tester throughput can offset that need. Product test adds high-speed interconnect/storage exposure, while collaborative robotics has different adoption and margin dynamics. Platform software and applications engineering support customer qualification, but dual-vendor strategies benefit rivals as well. More AI capex does not map one-for-one into tester revenue.

Advantest's October 28 primary semiannual release reports ¥845.2 billion revenue, up 60.5%, and ¥456.8 billion operating income. Its strong test demand corroborates the cycle, but also confirms a capable rival; yen/IFRS and product mix make direct margin ranking unreliable. FormFactor's October 28 release reports capacity investment and a Teradyne collaboration, showing ecosystem opportunity without removing execution or minority-investment risk. [Advantest current results](https://www.advantest.com/document/en/investors/ir-library/result/E_FR_FY2026_2Q.pdf), [FormFactor results](https://investors.formfactor.com/static-files/7dcd844f-b366-4078-a8d2-79dc63dc4eed).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.

## What could break the thesis

Test time, device complexity and market share can move in opposite directions. Customer program delays, export controls, a memory investment pause or a rival design win can quickly reduce equipment utilization and gross margin. Robotics profitability is new and cannot be treated as a proven steady-state return. The FormFactor commitment reduces near-term liquidity, while the minority put value can rise with performance. Watch compute customer ramps, memory mix, orders, working-capital conversion and Q4 margin against guidance; persistent misses would invalidate the base growth path.
