## How the business creates value

Chipmakers and outsourced test houses buy systems to detect defects, meet performance specifications and improve usable yield. Higher complexity, advanced packaging and HBM can increase test time per device; better tester throughput can offset that need. Product test adds high-speed interconnect/storage exposure, while collaborative robotics has different adoption and margin dynamics. Platform software and applications engineering support customer qualification, but dual-vendor strategies benefit rivals as well. More AI capex does not map one-for-one into tester revenue.

Advantest's October 28 primary semiannual release reports ¥845.2 billion revenue, up 60.5%, and ¥456.8 billion operating income. Its strong test demand corroborates the cycle, but also confirms a capable rival; yen/IFRS and product mix make direct margin ranking unreliable. Form Factor's October 28 release reports capacity investment and a Teradyne collaboration, showing ecosystem opportunity without removing execution or minority-investment risk. [Advantest current results](https://www.advantest.com/document/en/investors/ir-library/result/E_FR_FY2026_2Q.pdf), [Form Factor results](https://investors.formfactor.com/static-files/7dcd844f-b366-4078-a8d2-79dc63dc4eed).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.
