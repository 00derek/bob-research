## Business economics and macro transmission

Customers choose reliable components that fit demanding designs, sometimes with high qualification costs relative to the component's price. Higher data rates and power density increase engineering needs. TE Connectivity and specialist suppliers also compete for these budgets; the opportunity is broader than one exclusive AI connector. Diversification helps, but IT datacom has become a large enough business that its order cycle matters substantially.

Third-quarter sales increased from **$6.194 billion to $10.230 billion**: **65% reported growth versus 37% organic**, with acquisitions providing much of the difference. Gross profit was **$4.016 billion**, operating income **$3.021 billion** and net income attributable to Amphenol **$2.034 billion**, compared with **$1.246 billion** a year earlier. Operating margin was **29.5%**. Orders of approximately **$12.2 billion** produced **1.19 book-to-bill**, supporting near-term demand rather than proving a permanent growth rate. Q4 guidance is **$10.3–10.5 billion sales and $0.81–0.83 adjusted EPS**. [October 28 issuer release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/820313/000110465926119421/aph-ex99d1.htm).

The call reports IT datacom sales up **117% reported and 77% organically**, with AI approximately three-quarters of that market's sales. Communications networks, however, declined **28% organically**. Acquisition growth and different market cycles therefore need separate interpretation. CommScope broadens fiber/connectivity capability, while the latest smaller deals expand sensors and related applications; future deal authorization is not already acquired revenue. A new $4 billion repurchase program and 20% dividend increase describe capital intentions, not completed future purchases. [Full October call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aph/transcripts/659916-q3-2026/).

TE's own results also describe increasing AI orders, corroborating industry demand while demonstrating competition. **Evidence gap:** Comparable current market shares, customer-level overlap and a synchronized estimate-revision panel were not verified.
