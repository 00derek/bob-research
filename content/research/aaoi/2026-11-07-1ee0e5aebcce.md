## Business economics and macro transmission

Cloud customers buy transceivers that turn electrical data into light, while cable operators buy HFC equipment for broadband upgrades. Qualification and integration provide some customer stickiness, and domestic production can diversify supply. Optical performance, cost, yields and delivery matter more than the fact that a product has a faster headline speed. Coherent, Lumentum and Asian module producers compete for the same budgets and components; demand growth alone does not establish pricing power.

**Evidence gap:** Independent competitor yields, customer share and a synchronized industry revision panel were not verified. The issuer's capacity and demand claims are differentiated from independent proof. Top-ten customers represented approximately 99% of first-half revenue, and purchase orders generally do not provide commitments beyond a year, increasing forecast sensitivity to a few buyers.

The November 5 release reports September-quarter revenue **$269.9 million**, versus $118.6 million a year earlier; GAAP gross margin **28.2%** and net income **$6.3 million**. Fourth-quarter guidance is **$385–420 million revenue**, **29.5–31% adjusted gross margin**, and **$0.48–0.63 adjusted EPS** using 96 million diluted units. Manufacturing equipment capacity approached 330,000 units per month; management now expects more than 500,000 at end 2026 and over 1 million at end 2027, with US expansion taking longer because of electrical-power/construction issues. These are equipment and guidance measures, not guaranteed shipments. [November 5 results](https://investors.ao-inc.com/2026-11-05-Applied-Optoelectronics-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results).
