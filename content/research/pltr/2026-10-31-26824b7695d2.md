## Investment thesis

Palantir turns disconnected enterprise and government data into operational decisions, with AI applications sitting on top of a common permissioned data model. The August quarter shows unusually fast growth, real GAAP operating profit and cash generation. This October 31 rebuild funds a substantially larger business and allows existing equity claims rather than treating the old unsupported model as current. The price still exceeds 115% of weighted value, producing a conditional 50% valuation TRIM. The favorable alternative is that AIP becomes durable operating infrastructure across many customers; the opposing case is that today’s scarce implementation advantage becomes easier to replicate while spending and tax normalize. A strong business can deliver disappointing returns from an expensive starting price.

## How the business creates value

Foundry and AIP serve commercial operations; Gotham and related platforms support government decisions. Ontology, permissions and deployment knowledge can make switching costly, but adoption must translate into retained, paid use. In Q2, US commercial revenue was $764 million, US government $809 million, with international commercial/government about $182/$181 million. US commercial customers reached 653, up 35%, while revenue rose 149%; expanding usage at existing accounts contributed materially. Net dollar retention of 157% and $124 million average trailing revenue among the top 20 customers support expansion but also concentration. [Issuer business update](https://investors.palantir.com/files/Palantir%20-%20Q2%202026%20Business%20Update.pdf).

Microsoft’s September 28 primary announcement advances Fabric IQ and a shared business ontology alongside its broader data/AI distribution. Native platform integration is a credible alternative for some customers and can also coexist with Palantir. The announcement contains no comparable win-rate, operating margin or displacement evidence, so the model credits implementation and existing expansion without assuming an unchallenged monopoly. [Microsoft data announcement](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/28/new-microsoft-data-innovations-unlock-what-only-your-business-knows/).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.

## What could break the thesis

Customer implementation complexity, procurement controversy, government concentration and data rights can slow conversion. AIP pilots and cancellable deal value can fail to turn into RPO and cash. High current retention can fall as competitors improve or customers optimize spending. Investment marks, low tax and compensation exclusions can obscure recurring profit if treated as permanent. Rebuild on declining retained usage, deteriorating cash conversion or higher normalized costs. A multiple contraction can overwhelm operational growth even if the business thesis remains intact.

Business-quality gate **PASS at 28/30**; credibility gate **PASS**; no deep-value exception. Pre-profit flag **No**, based on current reported profitability rather than the historical label. Binary/event risk is **HIGH**; plausible gap stress **30%** is an analyst stress, not a maximum loss.

The chosen stress is larger than that sample's overnight decline, allowing for unobserved future events. Corporate actions and vendor history can distort samples, and even a long sample cannot bound a future loss.

**Evidence gap:** No exhaustive current legal/insider clearance or full event-loss distribution is established. Named funding and execution concerns limit confidence; the absence of an additional finding is not assurance of safety.
