## Business economics and macro transmission

Corning's manufacturing know-how, qualification, integration and customer relationships support differentiated products. Fiber count per computing system can rise even if GPU unit growth slows, while new high-density connections earn more revenue per unit of factory capacity. Display glass, automotive, industrial and solar businesses have distinct cycles; they do not all deserve the same AI growth rate. Customers can fund capacity upfront, but the cash must later be returned economically through deliveries or contractual terms.

Third-quarter GAAP revenue was **$4.855 billion versus $4.100 billion**, gross margin approximately **37.3%**, and operating profit **$911 million versus $589 million**. [October 27 primary results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000002474126000273/glw-20261027xex99xq32026.htm).

Carrier grew 4%, with data-center interconnect and long-term agreements supporting demand. Glass Innovations sales declined approximately 3% to $1.5 billion as memory costs pressured device markets. Solar sales grew 37%, but productivity ramped more slowly than expected. These are different economics, rather than one uniform high-growth segment.

The call guides Q4 core sales **$5.2–5.3 billion** and core EPS **$0.96–1.00**.
