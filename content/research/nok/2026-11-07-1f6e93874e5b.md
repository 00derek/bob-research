## Investment thesis

Optical and IP networking demand can improve the sales mix, if factories and working capital stop absorbing the benefit. The November 7 assessment is **TRIM** at the **$7.66 November 6 completed close**, versus **$7.31 weighted present value**, -4.6% potential return. The quote exceeds weighted value and remaining gap-stress reward/risk is -0.230:1, activating the conditional 75% valuation-reduction target. ## Business economics and financial evidence



### How the business creates value

Optical systems connect data centers and carrier networks; IP routers move traffic; radio and core systems serve operators; patent licensing monetizes the standards patent collection. Design qualification, installed networks and engineering support switching costs. Large customers can still split vendors, delay acceptance or press prices. A radio investment cycle is different from hyperscaler connectivity demand.

Q3 AI/cloud sales were €631 million, versus €279 million yearly: 123% growth at constant currencies, but only about 12% of total revenue. Telecommunications providers still supplied €3.640 billion, nearly 71% of sales, and were flat at constant currencies. IP Networks grew 43% and optical 18%, while fixed access remained weaker. Combining those growth rates into an all-company AI growth rate would overstate the transition. [Full September operating report](https://www.nokia.com/system/files/2026-10/nokia_results_2026_q3.pdf).

Management expects combined optical and IP sales to grow 25–30% in 2027. The call describes more than doubling sequential 800G pluggable shipments and 29 customer design wins. Those are company observations, not independently verified share gains or all recurring revenue. New IP products initially diluted gross margin; management expects scaling to improve it. Radio projects completed ahead of schedule moved some Q4 sales into Q3. The CEO nevertheless describes a broadly flat traditional telecom market and component costs rising across memory, semiconductors and other materials. Early shipments are not assumed to create unlimited incremental demand. [October 22 management call](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/hel/NOKIA/transcripts/460672-q3-2026/).

Ericsson's October 15 primary result reports SEK59.478 billion sales, 7% organic growth and SEK4.902 billion cash flow before M&A. It also describes a flat RAN market and higher component costs in 2027. Different currencies, mix, licensing and accounting prevent an equal-margin ranking; the evidence supports real competition and common cost pressure. Ciena, Huawei and other suppliers also contest optical or routing deployments; no exclusive AI infrastructure share is assumed. [Ericsson primary results](https://www.ericsson.com/en/press-releases/2026/10/ericsson-reports-third-quarter-results-2026).

**Evidence gap:** Product-level pricing, yields, customer overlap and synchronized peer revisions are incomplete. Supplier claims and design wins establish mechanisms, not the exact future market share. Paid shipments, accepted capacity and cash margins must validate the forecast.
