## Business economics and financial evidence

Unified telemetry and accumulated configuration can increase switching costs. Consumption pricing links revenue to monitored workloads, but also exposes growth to customer optimization and alternative tools. Arize adds AI evaluation and monitoring; integration must produce cross-selling and retention without duplicating engineering cost. Datadog, Cisco/Splunk, cloud-native monitoring and open-source telemetry remain credible alternatives. No independent market-share or price-premium advantage is assumed.
