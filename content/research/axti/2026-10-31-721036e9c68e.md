## Investment thesis

AXT supplies compound-semiconductor wafers used in optical communications and other specialized chips. Faster optical links can expand indium phosphide demand, and qualified manufacturing capacity can convert that demand into higher sales. The October 29 quarter provides stronger operating proof, but the October 28 Tongmei financing also changes the share of subsidiary economics attributable to AXT. The October 31 assessment is **PENDING REFRESH**, replacing AVOID while the post-event model is rebuilt. The latest completed close is **$55.06 on October 30**, independently corroborated by both historical price tables. No current fair value or entry zone is affirmed. The strongest opposing case is that improved yields and scarce supply make the growth worth much more than the old scenarios; that case must include minority ownership, financing conditions and the cash needed to expand capacity. Consolidated revenue growth alone cannot establish value per AXT common share.

## How the business creates value

Optical-device makers use indium phosphide substrates where silicon cannot provide the required optoelectronic performance. Crystal quality, yields, qualification and reliable supply can create customer value. AXT also participates in raw-material companies, supporting supply access but adding minority-interest and geographic complexity. Faster AI links increase the opportunity; inventory cycles, competing substrates, new supply and customer concentration can limit pricing power.

The base operating question is whether added capacity is accepted by customers and earns cash after reinvestment. Announced factory capacity is not the same as saleable output, and a substrate shortage may ease before a new facility earns its expected return. The next forecast needs separate volume, price, yield and attributable-margin assumptions.

## Financial health and capital allocation

The October 29 Q3 release reports revenue of $73.4 million, against $47.6 million in Q2 and $28.0 million a year earlier. Gross margin reached 56.0% and attributable GAAP earnings were $25.7 million, or $0.39 diluted EPS. Diluted weighted-average shares were 65.862 million. The quarter therefore strengthens the operating trajectory, while its weighted-average share count is not a terminal fully diluted claim count.

The balance sheet reports $485.472 million cash, $41.200 million restricted cash, $27.266 million short-term investments and $271.956 million long-term investments. Restricted cash and investments are not automatically freely distributable parent cash. Capacity, working capital and subsidiary obligations must be reconciled before adding any surplus cash to equity value.

The October 28 financing notice describes a conditional $285–545 million investment directly into Tongmei, with December 2026 expected closing. At the maximum investment, AXT expects approximately 73.40% ownership and continued consolidation. These are prospective terms, not completed proceeds or unchanged parent ownership. Continued accounting consolidation does not mean common shareholders retain 100% of subsidiary profit.

**Evidence gap:** The full financing agreements, redemption rights, post-close ownership waterfall, subsidiary cash restrictions, capex commitments and multi-period cash conversion are not reconciled. A current financial score cannot substitute for that claim bridge. The prior 17/30 fundamentals assessment remains a provisional quality judgment, supported by the now-profitable operating trend but limited by allocation and ownership uncertainties.

## What could break the thesis

New capacity or alternative materials could remove scarcity before expansion earns an adequate return. Customer qualification delays, poor yields, export restrictions and cash trapped in subsidiaries could separate reported growth from parent distributions. The strongest favorable countercase is sustained shortage pricing and rapid successful qualification; it would be supported by recurring attributable earnings and cash collections rather than factory announcements.

Quality remains provisionally **PASS** on the prior 17/30 evidence assessment; credibility **PASS** within the reviewed disclosures. A disclosed subsidiary financing is a modeling problem and is not itself a verified fraud or governance violation. Pre-profit flag **No** reflects the latest profitable quarter; binary risk remains **HIGH** because financing and volatile operating outcomes are unresolved. The **35%** adverse-gap assumption is a planning stress, not a guaranteed maximum loss or an execution promise.

**Evidence gap:** Current complete insider/short/institutional-flow histories, litigation changes and event-gap distributions are not reconciled. Watch attributable profit, ownership, cash restrictions, customer acceptance and redemption/closing conditions. Any verified thesis-breaking or credibility event requires immediate reassessment.
