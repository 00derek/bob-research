## Investment thesis

Nebius builds and rents AI computing infrastructure. Large customer commitments can improve utilization and support financing, but common equity earns the residual after GPUs, power, construction, debt, prepayments and replacement investment. Demand is therefore only the first step in the investment case. The October 22 $3.5 billion secured facility helps near-term funding, yet its 364-day maturity and floating cost add refinancing sensitivity rather than proving an attractive per-unit return. At the $182.34 October 28 completed close, the assessment is **Pending Refresh** because the current common-equity model is incomplete. The former $221.95 weighted estimate is suspended; it cannot be used to call the stock cheap after a material capital event. The strongest opposing case is that contracted demand creates rapid utilization and financing pays for itself. A reconciled fully funded buildout, credible cash conversion and diluted common-equity model would be required to establish that case.

## How the business creates value

AI labs and enterprises pay for compute capacity and supporting software. Nebius must secure power, GPUs, data-center availability and reliable operations before those contracts become revenue. Early prepayments can reduce funding strain, but they also bring delivery obligations. GPU depreciation and replacement make cash economics different from a low-capital software business; concentration in a few large buyers and vendors raises execution sensitivity.

The [August 12 Q2 financials](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094568/tm2622968d1_ex99-1.htm) show $582.3 million revenue, a $190.4 million continuing GAAP loss, about $5.657 billion property/intangible purchases and $8.042 billion unrestricted cash. Non-current debt was about $8.499 billion before later financing. The earlier August $5.75 billion gross convertible issue is a common-equity claim sensitivity, not costless cash.

The [October 22 issuer announcement](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-secures-3-5-billion-financing-facility-with-citi) specifies a 364-day secured facility initially at TermSOFR+2.75%, rising to as much as3.50% plus adjustments. It is availability, not proof of a full draw. Management cites more than $40 billion long-term customer commitments, but contract value is neither present equity value nor free cash. **Evidence gap:** Drawn debt, collateral, covenants, replacement capex, customer advances and convertible outcomes have not been reconciled into one funded common-equity bridge. Credibility is PASS on the materials reviewed: no distinct substantiated fraud, governance or unexplained-dilution event was established. The unresolved capital interpretation blocks valuation, and ordinary disclosed borrowing alone is not a credibility failure.

## Macro transmission

Higher rates feed directly into floating debt and refinancing, besides discounting distant residual earnings. Energy and equipment inflation affect construction and GPU-service margins. Risk liquidity can enable a buildout but may retreat before refinancing. AI compute demand is secular, while customers' financing and capacity utilization are cyclical. Power access, export controls and jurisdictional rules can delay deployment.

The October 28 shared macro assessment is **9/25**: rates 1/5, inflation 1/5, liquidity 4/5, cycle 2/5 and policy 1/5. The Fed maintained its policy range at 3.75–4.00%; the official 10-year Treasury par yield was 5.05%. This remains an uptrend under pressure with a 50% normal-risk sizing band and starter/add stages only. Macro changes permitted risk, rather than mechanically adding to company conviction or imposing a second market-direction multiplier. Brent observations conflict by contract/clock, and October 29 GDP/PCE were not yet released.

**Evidence gap:** The five economic channels above are qualitative sensitivities; no verified company-specific elasticity to rates, energy or liquidity is claimed. Quantify those effects in the operating scenarios if they materially change margins, financing or demand.

## What could break the thesis

The failure case is capacity that becomes available late, is underutilized or earns less than its financing and replacement cost. A short-dated secured facility can move the funding deadline ahead of the economic payoff. Watch actual commissioning, cash collections net of delivery obligations, financing maturities, dilution and customer concentration. The positive countercase requires realized high utilization and improving recurring cash after investment. The 35% adverse-gap stress recognizes funding and execution sensitivity; no defensible current structural stop is asserted, and an arbitrary tighter line would not solve the risk.
