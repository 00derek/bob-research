## Investment thesis

Nebius has real AI-cloud demand and a rapidly expanding revenue base. The common-equity question is whether it can finance, commission and replace enough infrastructure without surrendering too much value to lenders and new shares. The rebuilt security assessment is **TRIM** at the October 30 close of $154.14, versus probability-weighted present value **$114.93**. Credibility is **PENDING** while material financial-control weaknesses and remediation remain unresolved; this is not a fraud finding. The opposing case is powerful: sustained utilization and financing could turn contracted capacity into enormous recurring earnings, producing the $416.05 bull estimate. The new model explicitly prices that possibility alongside a 40% zero-equity funding-failure case. It replaces the previous whole-model PENDING valuation with an auditable, highly conditional capital plan.

## How the business creates value

Nebius designs and operates GPU infrastructure and a software layer for AI training, inference and related services. Customers pay for usable compute capacity, not for a signed power reservation. Hardware/software integration, deployment speed and contracted power can differentiate it. Hyperscalers and other specialist clouds compete for the same customers, equipment, power and financing. Customer prepayments can fund construction, but also create future service obligations and concentration risk.

The latest released quarter is **Q2, published August 12**, not an assumed September result. The directly reviewed [SEC earnings statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926094568/tm2622968d1_ex99-1.htm) provide the following USD billions:

These channels explain the 18% equity required return, future-financing dilution and substantial failure probability rather than being a separate decorative risk list.

## What could break the thesis

Failure can arise from power delays, commissioning bottlenecks, customers postponing utilization, insufficient prepayments, loss of refinancing access, or equipment becoming uneconomic faster than depreciation assumptions. Lenders can hold liens on assets while new equity absorbs dilution. Revenue growth can coexist with common-equity impairment. The financial-control weaknesses matter because asset accounting and depreciation are central to terminal profit. Credibility remains **PENDING**, prohibiting new capital until their scope/remediation is sufficiently resolved; the reviewed disclosures do not justify declaring fraud or automaticFAIL.

Binary event risk is **HIGH**, pre-profit statusYes, and plausible gap stress 35%. That stress is not a maximum loss; a funding failure can erase common value. Evidence that would strengthen the case includes reported cash after customer obligations, timely capacity-to-billing conversion, durable utilization, a fully funded construction schedule and verified remediation. Thesis deterioration would include a funding gap without credible replacement capital or persistent sales failing to cover operating and replacement costs.
