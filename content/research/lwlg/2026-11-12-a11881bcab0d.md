## Business economics and competitive position

A nonexclusive materials/license model could earn from recurring material shipments, royalties and engineering work. The license and polymer supply are interdependent under ASC 606, so they are one performance obligation rather than two independent full revenue streams. June-quarter sales came from one Swiss license customer. A $100,000 development advance remained a contract liability; no NRE revenue was recognized in the first half. Technical engagement is therefore distinct from accepted development revenue and paid high-volume output. [Primary June filing and revenue-recognition notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1325964/000107997326001097/lwlg_10q-063026.htm).
