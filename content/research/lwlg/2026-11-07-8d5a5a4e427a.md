## How the business creates value

A nonexclusive materials/license model could earn from recurring material shipments, royalties and engineering work. The license and polymer supply are interdependent under ASC 606, so they are one performance obligation rather than two independent full revenue streams. June-quarter sales came from one Swiss license customer. A $100,000 development advance remained a contract liability; no NRE revenue was recognized in the first half. Technical engagement is therefore distinct from accepted development revenue and paid high-volume output. [Primary June filing and revenue-recognition notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1325964/000107997326001097/lwlg_10q-063026.htm).

NVIDIA’s June Computex primary announcement says Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics is in full production with TSMC/SPIL/TFC/Foxconn manufacturing relationships. This demonstrates real demand for optical scaling and established alternatives reaching shipment. That June release does not name Lightwave as a supplier. The later October 29 Foxconn demonstration is now separate, direct integration evidence; neither announcement establishes paid high-volume polymer shipments. The model assigns no hyperscaler revenue solely from thematic association. [Primary optical production announcement](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-gtc-taipei-computex-2026-news/).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.

The October 29 issuer announcement identifies a joint Foxconn silicon-organic-hybrid optical-engine/transceiver demonstration at Hon Hai Tech Day, targeting 200 G and 400 G per lane. This is more concrete system integration than anonymous prototype counts, but the release explicitly leaves testing and qualification ongoing. It does not disclose paid production volumes, customer acceptance revenue, yield, contractual pricing or a commercial supply award. The six-program base forecast is retained as an assumption rather than increased on the demonstration alone.
