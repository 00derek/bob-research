## Investment thesis

Lightwave develops electro-optic polymers intended to improve optical links while relying on customer and foundry manufacturing rather than owning a full wafer fab. The latest released June quarter shows customer-development progress and a funded research runway, but only tens of thousands of dollars of sales. This new October 31 model makes the volume, pricing, dilution and cash assumptions explicit. AVOID follows the failed business-quality floor and weak probability-weighted value. The favorable alternative is that qualified customer programs produce recurring material and royalty income with attractive incremental economics. The opposing case is that established silicon-photonics alternatives reach volume first, leaving prototypes, qualification costs and repeated equity funding without durable common profit.

## How the business creates value

A nonexclusive materials/license model could earn from recurring material shipments, royalties and engineering work. The license and polymer supply are interdependent under ASC 606, so they are one performance obligation rather than two independent full revenue streams. June-quarter sales came from one Swiss license customer. A $100,000 development advance remained a contract liability; no NRE revenue was recognized in the first half. Technical engagement is therefore distinct from accepted development revenue and paid high-volume output. [Primary June filing and revenue-recognition notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1325964/000107997326001097/lwlg_10q-063026.htm).

NVIDIA’s June Computex primary announcement says Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics is in full production with TSMC/SPIL/TFC/Foxconn manufacturing relationships. This demonstrates real demand for optical scaling and established alternatives reaching shipment. It does not name Lightwave as a supplier, prove polymer adoption, or prove that no polymer product can compete later. The model assigns no hyperscaler revenue solely from thematic association. [Primary optical production announcement](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-gtc-taipei-computex-2026-news/).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.
