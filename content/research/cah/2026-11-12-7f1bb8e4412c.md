The **November 12, 2026 assessment is TRIM**, at the **$277.45 November 11 completed close**, versus **$144.18 probability-weighted present value (-48.0%)**. The current price triggers the conditional 50% valuation-reduction target.

Those values depend on explicit forecasts and capital claims; they are not price targets inferred from a rally. The November 11 ordinary dividend declaration does not increase earning power. ### How a narrow margin becomes profit

The pharmaceutical business purchases and distributes branded, generic and specialty drugs and supplies related services. Distribution scale, reliable delivery, working-capital management and generic sourcing support customer relationships. Specialty physician services can earn more profit per revenue dollar, but acquisitions and physician incentives cost money. Global Medical Products and Distribution manufactures and distributes medical products; home-based care, nuclear medicine and other growth businesses provide different margins and demand drivers. Drug-price inflation or expensive GLP-1 products can raise revenue sharply without a matching percentage increase in distributable profit.

The [November 5 release](https://newsroom.cardinalhealth.com/2026-11-05-Cardinal-Health-Reports-First-Quarter-Fiscal-Year-2027-Results-and-Raises-Outlook) reports the September 30 quarter. Pharma sales were$62.582 billion, up 6%, and segment profit$783 million, up 17%, a 1.25% margin. Medical sales were$3.190 billion, essentially flat, and profit fell 28% to$33 million, a 1.03% margin. Other sales$1.764 billion grew 7% and profit$193 million grew 16%, a 10.94% margin. These segment profits precede corporate and excluded costs; summing them is not common earnings. Segment sales also require intersegment elimination before comparison with consolidated$67.513 billion revenue.

The [October 1 binding letter of intent](https://newsroom.cardinalhealth.com/2026-10-01-Cardinal-Health-Enters-into-Binding-Letter-of-Intent-to-Extend-Pharmaceutical-Distribution-Agreements-with-CVS-Health) proposed extending pharmaceutical distribution with CVS through June 2032. The November 5 release continues to describe the binding letter of intent; final definitive completion is not established in retrieved primary evidence. This improves volume visibility with a large customer but does not disclose a fixed margin on every future unit. The separate Red Oak generic sourcing venture runs through June 2029 and requires quarterly CVS payments, according to the September 10-Q. Both relationships create dependence as well as scale.

Competition is profitable and capable. [McKesson's November 4 primary release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/927653/000092765326000279/mck_exhibit991x9302026.htm) reports$111.0 billion quarterly revenue,8% growth, and a raised adjusted EPS outlook. Different mixes, fiscal periods and adjustments prevent an equal-margin ranking. The comparison supports healthcare distribution demand and demonstrates that specialty opportunity is not exclusive to Cardinal.
