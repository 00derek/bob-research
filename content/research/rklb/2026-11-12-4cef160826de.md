## Business economics and competitive position

Electron/HASTE sell launch access; spacecraft and components sell mission capability. Neutron opens larger payloads but adds development and reusable-launch execution risk. Iridium brings spectrum, distribution and service subscriptions whose replacement-capital requirements differ from manufacturing. Vertical integration can reduce external launch expense while creating dependence on internal schedules. SpaceX is a capable integrated competitor; government programs can favor second-source capability without guaranteeing a particular share or margin. Internal launches must not be double-counted as external revenue. The combined forecast separates launch, systems and communications before calculating equity profit.
