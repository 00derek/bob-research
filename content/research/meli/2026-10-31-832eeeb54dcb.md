## Investment thesis

MercadoLibre's commerce, logistics, payments and credit businesses reinforce one another, but rapid growth currently consumes margin and funding. The deep rebuild retains a **BUY_STARTER security candidate** at the October 30 close of **$1,624.53**, with **$2,321.68** weighted present value and 42.9% upside. This uses the previously declared $1,500–1,640 accumulation zone and $1,450 structural closing stop, which remain unbreached; it does not create a new zone retroactively. Reward/risk is 3.99:1. Weak momentum restricts further scaling. The strongest opposing case is that competition, credit costs and regulatory funding requirements prevent engagement gains from producing sufficient common profit; the bear estimate is $531.56. A security candidate is conditional and does not assert any transaction.

## How the business creates value

The marketplace connects buyers and sellers; logistics improve delivery and conversion; advertising monetizes purchase intent; Mercado Pago extends payment, savings and lending services beyond marketplace transactions. Shared distribution and customer data can reduce acquisition costs and improve underwriting. The economic question is whether those benefits outweigh shipping subsidies, lower seller fees, credit losses and the capital needed to support larger balances. Payment volume, assets managed for customers and credit principal are not all company revenue or shareholder cash.

The [actual June 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1099590/000109959026000023/meli-20260630.htm) and [earnings Q&A](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/meli/transcripts/662711-q2-2026/) show why margin recovery requires judgment. Lower seller take rates and Pix discounts in Brazil attract supply and demand, while shipping, energy and payment-device costs consume part of scale gains. Management chose not to pass through all costs and described some device inventory losses upfront. Benefits must emerge through cohort maturation and repeat behavior, not just sales growth. The issuer does not provide traditional near-term earnings guidance.

Competition remains active. [Sea's issuer-distributed August results](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sea-limited-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-2026-08-11) show Shopee revenue 4.9 billion, up 48.9%, with continued logistics investment. Those are global figures, not a direct Brazil-only market-share denominator. Shopee, Amazon, local retailers, banks and payment firms can compete on prices, service or funding even as the regional digital market expands. MercadoLibre's established logistics and integrated services support a strong moat; they do not eliminate the cost of defending it.

### Credit growth needs capital and liquidity

The gross credit business exceeded 16 billion, up 75% year over year. Consolidated net interest margin after losses was 20.7%, improving sequentially but two points below a year earlier. Credit-card NIMAL was−2.5%, versus breakeven, as new cohorts expanded. The issuer's 15–90-day delinquency ratio was 7.0% overall and 4.6% for cards. These are defined delinquency windows, not total eventual credit losses or proof new cohorts will remain benign through a downturn. Credit losses 1.276 billion in Q2 are already operating expense; the model does not deduct them again after starting with common profit.

Funding has distinct claims. June cash 3.649 billion is separate from 13.114 billion restricted cash; funds payable to customers 16.035 billion are liabilities. Current/noncurrent financial liabilities total 10.626 billion, including deposits/bills, bank funding, secured financing, corporate notes and finance leases. Operating leases add 2.550 billion. The issuer's available cash/investment definition is 6.751 billion, versus 13.176 billion total debt including operating leases:6.425 billion net debt. These categories cannot be replaced with 16.763 billion cash-flow-statement cash as unrestricted surplus.

Note 11 describes consolidated securitizations as secured funding. Third-party investors generally have recourse to specified assets, while MercadoLibre retains subordinated economic interests and residual losses. Ringfenced cash cannot simply be distributed to common equity. Short funding and the credit book need ongoing collections, deposits, secured issuance and bank capacity. Regulatory capital and local cash restrictions can bind before consolidated accounting equity is exhausted.

The long-term retention program is payable in **cash**, with 165 million first-half accrued compensation. It is not standard stock dilution to be ignored, nor a permanent free operating-cash addback. Current common units 50.6968 million and diluted 50.6973 million show little present dilution; forecasts use 52/51.5/51 million bear/base/bull claims to allow additional funding/award uncertainty. No preferred or separately material minority claim was identified in the reviewed balance sheet.

## Macro transmission

Local interest rates affect consumer credit, deposit pricing and NIMAL; global rates affect valuation and access to capital. Inflation affects shipping, wages and nominal demand. Liquidity sustains credit growth and refinancing; the consumer cycle changes spending and delinquency. Currency, tax reform, capital controls and financial-services regulation alter translated profit and distributable cash. These are reflected in slower bear growth, lower margins, higher tax and funding-capital requirements.

Macro is **8/25: rates 1, inflation 1, liquidity 2, cycle 3, policy 1**, with 50% normal-size cap and starter/add-only increases.
