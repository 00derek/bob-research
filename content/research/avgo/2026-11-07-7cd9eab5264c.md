## Investment thesis

Broadcom sells custom compute, networking chips and infrastructure software into customers whose switching and qualification costs can support attractive returns. The **November 7, 2026 security assessment is WATCH**, using the **November 6 completed close $293.69**. A newly underwritten operating/capital model gives **$378.38 weighted present value**, **+28.8% price return**. Customer ability to pay, rack resale values and the mix of AI hardware with software drive realized cash. The strongest opposing case is that AI customers' financing needs indicate demand beyond their ability to pay, while memory content and rack commitments dilute Broadcom's economics. The previously considered 285–300 band fails the chosen 25% adverse-gap reward/risk test and is withdrawn; a fresh supported setup and subsequent qualifying close would be required. ## Business economics and macro transmission

Custom accelerators can improve performance and power consumption for a customer's specific workloads; networking connects those systems, while enterprise software generates recurring revenue. Design integration and qualification create switching costs, but a small set of hyperscalers and AI laboratories controls large purchasing budgets. Growth depends on completed designs, foundry/package capacity, memory, power and customer finance—not just announced compute demand.

September 2 results cover the quarter ending **August 2**, not September. Revenue rose from **$15.952 billion to $29.591 billion**, operating income from **$5.887 billion to $15.955 billion**, and GAAP net income from **$4.140 billion to $13.088 billion**. AI semiconductor revenue reached **$16.7 billion**, versus a **$21.7 billion fourth-quarter outlook**; total fourth-quarter revenue guidance is **$34.8 billion**. Management's $115 billion fiscal 2027 and $230 billion fiscal 2028 AI trajectory is unusually large and supply-backed according to management, but is not a guarantee. [Issuer results](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial); [September call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/09/09/broadcom-avgo-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/).

Mix matters. The call's non-GAAP gross margin falls from about **75% to 73%** in the fourth-quarter outlook as memory-intensive accelerators expand, while non-GAAP operating margin is guided to **66%**. The new model therefore does not simply apply the reported quarter's net margin to the entire 2028 revenue trajectory. Nvidia's integrated platforms and alternative custom-chip suppliers contest these budgets; customers can also redesign internally. Software licensing changes invite customer migration and regulatory challenge. **Evidence gap:** Independent supplier-share, customer-return and synchronized peer-revision tables were not verified. Management's demand visibility and competitor/platform announcements are useful evidence, not proof of a permanent monopoly.
