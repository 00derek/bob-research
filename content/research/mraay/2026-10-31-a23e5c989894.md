## Investment thesis

Murata sells the capacitors, filters, modules and power components that make electronic systems smaller, more reliable and more efficient. AI servers can increase high-capacitance content, adding a growth engine beside smartphones, vehicles and industrial devices. Manufacturing expertise and customer qualification support the business, but they do not eliminate consumer cycles or currency effects. At the $19.73 October 30 ADR close, the assessment is **Pending Refresh**. October 30 half-year results and raised full-year guidance supersede the prior operating basis. The old $17.39 weighted value is suspended and cannot establish a present trim or entry. The most important dependencies are sustained component margins and the yen-dollar rate used to translate ordinary earnings into ADR value. Stronger component demand supports the business, but the updated cash, share-count and FX assumptions still require a consistent per-ADR model before declaring new value.

## How the business creates value

The [October 30 half-year results](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/murata-manufacturing-second-quarter-of-fy2026-ce785fdad080f527), distributed issuer material linked from the [issuer IR index](https://corporate.murata.com/en-us/ir), show revenue ¥1.091 trillion and operating profit ¥245.9 billion, up 20.8% and 48.9%; component mix and utilization helped while weaker yen also contributed. Management raised March 2027 sales guidance to ¥2.26 trillion and attributable profit to ¥396 billion. Common shares excluding treasury were approximately 1.810 billion at September 30. The new evidence strengthens growth but makes the previous valuation basis stale. **Evidence gap:** Reconcile revised earnings, cash after investment, diluted units, ADR ratio and synchronized FX into fresh scenarios before any entry or valuation-trim conclusion.

Multilayer ceramic capacitors store and release charge in compact electronic assemblies; filters and RF modules control interference and communications. Ceramic processes, miniaturization and qualification can support customer retention. Higher server power and component content create opportunity, while smartphones, batteries and standard components remain exposed to inventory, pricing and substitution.

The [July 31 Q1 financial statement](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/murata-manufacturing-first-quarter-of-fy2026-ce7f50dbda8af127) reports ¥502.3 billion revenue, up 20.7%, ¥98.5 billion operating profit and ¥81.4 billion attributable profit. The issuer's year ending March 2027 guide was revenue ¥2.11 trillion and owner profit ¥338 billion. Earlier June cash around ¥557.5 billion against minimal borrowing supported solvency, but investment cash use and working capital mean profit and free cash are not interchangeable. These figures are historical July/June evidence, superseded where the October 30 half-year release provides current information.

The model's FY2027 label denotes the operating year endingMarch2028. Its base revenue ¥2.3 trillion is about 9% above the superseded July March 2027 guide, with 17% net margin and 1.75 billion ordinary diluted units. [Citi's current depositary page](https://depositaryreceipts.citi.com/adr/notices/pgm_dispCA.aspx?cusip=626425102&pageId=15&subpageID=112) confirms one ordinary to two ADRs. **Evidence gap:** Current product/end-market margins, net cash after reinvestment, common-unit buybacks and a synchronous Tokyo/ADR/FX valuation are incomplete; the U.S. ADR close is not substituted for the local-session close.

Rates affect the discount on future component profits and customer electronics spending. Inflation changes energy/material costs and consumer budgets; liquidity influences inventory financing. AI content growth coexists with cyclical smartphones and vehicles. Yen changes affect both operating earnings and dollar translation, while trade policy and production geography can alter access and costs.

The October 31 shared macro assessment is **8/25**: rates 1/5, inflation 1/5, liquidity 2/5, cycle 3/5 and policy 1/5. ## What could break the thesis

The strongest opposing case to caution is that AI component intensity and pricing persist longer than the model assumes. The failure case is that smartphone or battery weakness offsets AI growth, excess industry capacity compresses pricing, or the yen translation overwhelms operating gains. Watch segment order quality, utilization, margins after new capacity and cash after investment. Two ADRs per ordinary and local/U.S. market timing must remain consistent. An18% adverse overnight gap is an assumption, not an executable protection level.
