## Investment thesis

TSMC manufactures the silicon behind many competing AI designs, so it can earn returns across customers rather than needing to select a single winning chip. At the **$375.44 November 6 completed close**, the November 7 re-underwritten model gives **$434.43 probability-weighted present value (+15.7%)**. The security assessment is **WATCH**. The opposing case combines lower customer returns on AI infrastructure with higher depreciation, overseas costs or an interruption in Taiwan. Good chips do not eliminate concentrated physical risk.

## How the business creates value

Customers pay for manufactured wafers, packaging and engineering services. Leading-edge process yield and dependable delivery make switching expensive, but customers can redesign or split future programs. Q3 shipments were 4.476 million twelve-inch-equivalent wafers, up only 9.6% yearly while revenue grew much faster; mix and value per wafer, rather than unit growth alone, drove economics. HPC was 68% of sales and smartphones 21%; advanced nodes were 79% of wafer revenue, including 8% at 2nm and 33% at 3nm. These are revenue shares, not physical-capacity percentages. [October 15 operating tables](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-10/f83780000ddc0c3b48e57a87eab6814c4ab35058/3Q26%20ManagementReport.pdf).

Samsung reports improving foundry revenue/earnings and new 2nm HPC orders from major hyperscalers in its October 29 release. Those orders demonstrate a credible alternative, but do not establish equal yields or disclose a directly comparable foundry profit margin. TSMC customer trust and packaging integration support a moat; neither prevents competitive pricing or customer diversification. [Samsung primary Q3 release](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-third-quarter-2026-results).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.
