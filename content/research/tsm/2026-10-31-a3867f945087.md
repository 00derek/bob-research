## Investment thesis

TSMC manufactures the silicon behind many competing AI designs, so it can earn returns across customers rather than needing to select a single winning chip. October 15 results show strong leading-edge demand and cash conversion. The new October 31 model explicitly funds a larger overseas fab base and normalizes profitability below the current quarter. WATCH follows the limited expected return after discounting that growth and allowing for a wide ordinary-cycle bear case and separate geopolitical loss risk. The favorable case is that process leadership, packaging and customer qualification keep capacity valuable. The opposing case combines lower customer returns on AI infrastructure with higher depreciation, overseas costs or an interruption in Taiwan. Good chips do not eliminate concentrated physical risk.

## How the business creates value

Customers pay for manufactured wafers, packaging and engineering services. Leading-edge process yield and dependable delivery make switching expensive, but customers can redesign or split future programs. Q3 shipments were 4.476 million twelve-inch-equivalent wafers, up only 9.6% yearly while revenue grew much faster; mix and value per wafer, rather than unit growth alone, drove economics. HPC was 68% of sales and smartphones 21%; advanced nodes were 79% of wafer revenue, including 8% at 2 nm and 33% at 3 nm. These are revenue shares, not physical-capacity percentages. [October 15 operating tables](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-10/f83780000ddc0c3b48e57a87eab6814c4ab35058/3Q26%20ManagementReport.pdf).

Samsung reports improving foundry revenue/earnings and new 2 nm HPC orders from major hyperscalers in its October 29 release. Those orders demonstrate a credible alternative, but do not establish equal yields or disclose a directly comparable foundry profit margin. TSMC customer trust and packaging integration support a moat; neither prevents competitive pricing or customer diversification. [Samsung primary Q3 release](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-announces-third-quarter-2026-results).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.

## What could break the thesis

Geographic concentration, export rules, equipment availability, power and water constrain realized production. Customer AI returns can weaken before existing commitments expire. Overseas fabs and 2 nm/A14 ramps can reduce yield or increase depreciation faster than pricing. Watch monthly sales, node mix, wafer volume, overseas margin dilution and CFO after capex; a sustained demand or technology/yield miss requires rebuilding the growth and margin assumptions. Geopolitical loss can be discontinuous and larger than the modeled ordinary bear or gap stress.

Business-quality gate **PASS at 28/30**; credibility gate **PASS**; no deep-value exception. Pre-profit flag **No**, based on current reported profitability rather than the historical label. Binary/event risk is **HIGH**; plausible gap stress **35%** is an analyst stress, not a maximum loss.
