## Investment thesis

The October 30 ADR close now gives 25.07% upside to the October 20 model, but stop-based reward/risk is only 1.81:1 and price remains above the predeclared $385–400 starter zone. HOLD therefore persists: the improved valuation percentage alone does not qualify a starter. This is a price change, not a higher business forecast.

TSMC manufactures leading-edge chips for customers that design but do not operate comparable factories. Process technology, yields and packaging let customers build more powerful AI and other computing systems. Demand can benefit equity through high utilization, pricing and profit after investment. The important assumptions are continued leading-edge demand, durable margins and productive returns on large factory spending. The strongest opposing case is AI demand growing faster and longer than the base forecast. Taiwan concentration and costly geographic expansion remain major downside mechanisms even with healthy near-term orders.

## How the business creates value

TSMC earns wafer and packaging revenue from chip-design customers while focusing on manufacturing. Leading-edge processes can increase content per computing system, but capacity, yield and qualifications constrain conversion. New nodes can cannibalize older capacity; overseas fabs may reduce geographic dependence while diluting margins. The ADR represents underlying operating equity; this model uses ADR-equivalent diluted units, not the local ordinary-unit count.

October 15 Q3 results reported $46.72 billion revenue, 66.4% gross margin and 58.7% operating margin. Q4 guide is $52.3–54.3 billion revenue, 65–67% gross margin and 56.5–58.5% operating margin. These support strong demand and profit, while capex and customer/geographic concentration prevent treating current margins as perpetual. Cash must cover process and packaging investment before benefiting equity. [Primary evidence](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q3).

The October 20 FY2027 base uses $230 billion revenue and 52% net margin on 5.186 billion ADR-equivalent diluted units. Sales are 8% above the annualized Q4 guide midpoint; these are analyst earnings forecasts, not management FY2027 guidance. Bear $190 billion/44% margin stresses demand and overseas costs; bull $270 billion/56% needs exceptional leading-edge conversion. No newer issuer quarter was identified, so model date/inputs remain unchanged.
