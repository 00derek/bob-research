## Investment thesis

At the October 30 close of $399.81, PENDING_REFRESH persists. The post-Q3 automotive, energy and autonomy forecast still lacks a consistent reinvestment and equity-claim bridge; prospective combination discussions supply no agreed standalone valuation inputs.

Tesla sells vehicles, energy-storage systems and services while investing heavily in autonomy, AI and robotics. Software and energy could improve the earnings mix, but value for common equity depends on profitable adoption after capital spending and dilution. Better Q3 free cash flow does not establish the margins in older long-range cases. The strongest opposing case is that autonomy and AI produce durable, capital-efficient profits sooner than this cautious assessment allows. The test is actual commercial revenue and cash after investment, without treating a prospective corporate combination as completed.

## How the business creates value

Vehicles supply most current sales; energy and services have different growth/margin drivers. Software could increase revenue per installed vehicle, while autonomy and robotics require regulation, technical validation and capital. Manufacturing integration helps costs without proving profitable service-network economics. Distinct business forecasts are needed before adding their earnings into one equity value.

The October 21 Q3 update reports $28.046 billion revenue versus $28.095 billion a year earlier, $718 million operating income and $787 million GAAP net income. Operating cash flow was $6.263 billion, capex $4.732 billion and free cash flow $1.531 billion, improving from Q2 negative $1.092 billion. Cash and investments were $45.447 billion. These demonstrate liquidity, but weak current operating margin and equity-investment gains complicate earnings extrapolation. [Primary evidence](https://ir.tesla.com/_flysystem/s3/sec/000162828026066738/tsla-20261021-gen.pdf).

A replacement FY2030 model needs automotive, energy, services and autonomy/AI revenues separated; reinvestment, tax, financing and dilution treated consistently; and no double counting of potential SpaceX economics through both an investment and a hypothetical combination. No agreed combination terms are inserted. The old high-margin forecast remains suspended because the post-Q3 source-to-forecast and capital bridge is incomplete.
