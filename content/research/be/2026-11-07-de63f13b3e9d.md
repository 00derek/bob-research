## Investment thesis

Bloom Energy converts natural gas or other fuels into electricity using solid-oxide fuel cells. The **November 7, 2026 security assessment is TRIM**, using the **November 6 completed close $173.24**. A newly underwritten operating/capital model gives **$96.08 weighted present value**, **-44.5% price return**. The strongest opposing case is that power scarcity allows Bloom to sustain exceptionally rapid growth and high returns on incremental factory capital. Bloom sells equipment and services, with customer economics depending on gas delivery, electricity prices, reliability, emissions rules, maintenance and financing.
