## Investment thesis

Cloudflare sells security, network performance and developer infrastructure on a shared global network. More customers and products can improve network economics, while AI agents create potential demand for computing, storage and traffic control. Those opportunities must become common cash after compensation, equipment and financing. The October 31 assessment is **EXIT at $280.70**, the October 30 close. A new six-year cash-flow model gives **$48.32 weighted present value**, with **$121.46 in its operating bull case**. ## Business economics and growth

Application security, SASE/network services and Workers computing share infrastructure, developer relationships and distribution. That can make product bundling attractive and reduce customer complexity. Free traffic, paid traffic, AI inference and storage have different costs; developer registrations and requests do not translate automatically into revenue or constant gross margin. Hyperscalers, security platforms and other edge networks compete for the same customer budgets.

Second-quarter revenue increased from **$512.32 million to $696.06 million**, approximately **36%**. GAAP gross profit was **$499.52 million**, a **71.8% margin**, versus **74.9%** a year earlier. The call's **73.1% adjusted gross margin** is a different measure. Customers above $100, 000 annualized revenue increased from **3, 712 to 4, 698**, represented **73% of revenue**, and dollar-based net retention reached **120%**. These are substantial commercial proof points, while the large difference between user activity and monetization remains important. RPO reached **$2.732 billion**, up 38%, with approximately 64% current; not all RPO is immediate cash or next-quarter revenue. [Full August 6 call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/net/transcripts/662709-q2-2026/).

Management guided Q3 revenue **$736–737 million**, adjusted operating income **$129–130 million** and adjusted diluted EPS **$0.34** using approximately **374 million claims**. Full-year revenue guidance was **$2.864–2.870 billion**, adjusted operating income **$443–445 million** and adjusted EPS **$1.25–1.26**. The goal of GAAP profitability by 2028 at the latest is a dated management target. Customer anecdotes and new AI payment/content tools support product interest, but do not establish a separately disclosed, already profitable agent business.

The call expects network capex around **14–15% of 2026 revenue**, despite a lower spending quarter, and emphasizes whole-product unit economics as the mix changes. The new forecast includes both hardware and capitalized software rather than equating adjusted operating profit to cash distributable without investment. **Evidence gap:** Detailed product revenue, paid AI gross profit, complete cohort spend distributions and independent customer unit economics were not disclosed in the retrieved statements. The model's customer/spend bridge is an analyst requirement, not an invented segment report.

Zscaler's September 3 primary results report **$898.2 million quarterly revenue, 25% growth**, and **$3.771 billion ARR**, including an acquisition contribution. Its GAAP operating loss was $15.5 million despite adjusted operating profit of $218.4 million. This establishes active competing Zero Trust/security supply and shows that compensation adjustments matter across the industry. Cloudflare's network/developer mix differs, so the figures are not treated as a market-share panel or directly comparable margin ranking. **Evidence gap:** Synchronized industry revisions, independently verified product superiority and like-for-like returns remain unverified. [Zscaler primary results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713683/000171368326000156/zs-07312026_991.htm).

### Capitalization changes and governance

The June filing also describes **eight shareholder complaints as of August 5** concerning the approved capitalization plan. Seven consolidated complaints challenge fairness, founder control and liquidity benefits; the eighth challenges whether a two-thirds vote was required. The issuer and directors dispute the claims. These are unresolved allegations, not findings of fraud. Targeted post-June issuer/SEC searches did not establish a completed implementation notice, so the proposed structure is not silently treated as an accomplished October fact.

The plan would give each existing A/B common unit an additional non-voting **Class C** unit, while exchanging founder B units for A common plus **Series FF voting preferred**. The actual proxy gives Series FF nine votes, **no dividends**, and only **$0.001 liquidation preference per unit**, otherwise no economic rights. Its name therefore does not justify inventing a material senior cash claim. The ordinary split is described as preserving proportionate economic ownership while reallocating it between A and C. [Primary proxy and Series FF terms](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1477333/000119312526263809/d160227ddef14a.htm).

The model's **380 million existing economic units and $280.70 quote use the pre-change common-unit convention**. If implementation occurred, A/C values, awards and note conversion terms must be rebased consistently before exact per-unit use; neither total business value nor fair value should be mechanically doubled. A voting premium or non-voting discount could affect the allocation. **Evidence gap:** Implementation status, litigation resolution and any resulting A/C trading allocation were not verified. Credibility is **PENDING on this specific governance question**, with no conclusion that the financial statements are fraudulent. That unresolved issue independently prevents a fresh entry.
