The **November 12 assessment is TRIM**, at the **$29.14 November11 completed close**, versus a genuinely revised **$28.12 weighted present value (-3.5%)**. The November12 Kaynes term sheet adds real potential capital use before it produces proven profit. The model reserves the $56.4 million initial investment, gives no subsidy or sales premium, and expands bear claims for an explicit stress equity raise. Base cash can cover the initial outlay, but the bear needs financing. Faster product uptake could outperform the base; inventory reversal, metal costs or further venture funding could deepen the shortfall.

**Evidence gap:** JV definitive terms, construction timetable and later funding are unknown. The new model tests these claims without treating a conditional minority venture as fully consolidated revenue or assuming its government grant. It remains a conditional estimate, not an assured recovery.
