## Investment thesis

Vishay sells discrete semiconductors and passive components used across automotive, industrial and other electronics. Broad products and customer qualifications support repeat demand; recovering orders improve factory absorption and can lift profit faster than sales. HOLD at $29.92 on October 28 reflects that opportunity alongside only single-digit upside in the retained FY2027 weighted model. Price remains above the declared accumulation range, so no fresh entry is assessed. The main assumptions are backlog conversion, sustained pricing and cash margins after factory investment. The strongest opposing case is the recovery lasting longer and producing more profit than the base forecast. The downside is inventory/capacity turning again before earnings become durable.

## How the business creates value

Discrete devices manage signals/power; passive components serve many designs and markets. Electrification can increase content, but inventory/replacement cycles still affect price and volume. Product/customer breadth reduces dependence on one platform, while fixed manufacturing costs make utilization important. Technology and vertical production can improve costs/supply but require investment and execution.

August 5 Q2 results reported $888.6 million GAAP revenue, 23.3% gross margin and $0.19 diluted EPS. Book-to-bill was 1.32 and backlog 6.1 months. Q3 guide is $945–975 million revenue and 24%±0.5 points gross margin. A $30 million tariff-refund sales reduction did not reduce gross profit; GAAP/adjusted comparisons must be consistent. Orders support recovery, while full cash/debt/dilution reconciliation is less complete than revenue evidence. [Primary evidence](https://ir.vishay.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vishay-intertechnology-reports-second-quarter-2026-results).

The October 20 FY2027 base uses $4.2 billion sales, 6% net margin and 155 million diluted units, about 9% above annualized Q3 guide midpoint. That needs modest growth and cost absorption. Bear $3.6 billion/3% stresses inventory/pricing; bull $4.6 billion/9% needs a broad profitable upcycle. Model inputs retain their date and do not rise with the quote.

## What could break the thesis

Orders may build customer inventory rather than consumption; prices and utilization can then fall together. Auto/industrial weakness and tariffs can lower margins while investment continues. Sustained failure to turn an upcycle into cash or structural competitive losses would challenge the thesis. The favorable contrary case is broad demand making margin estimates conservative.

Thesis **INTACT**; credibility **PASS**; GAAP pre-profit **No**; binary-event risk **HIGH**. The 15% plausible overnight gap is an analyst stress, not a forecast bound. A daily-close stop cannot guarantee execution at its level. For HIGH binary risk, the larger of stop distance and gap stress governs sizing.

**Evidence gap:** Complete current gap distributions, material capital claims and synchronized insider/short evidence are unavailable. Test the specific business failures above and refresh immediately on results, financing, governance or thesis-changing news.
