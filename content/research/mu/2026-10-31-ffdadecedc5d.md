## How the business creates value

Micron makes DRAM, high-bandwidth memory and NAND storage. AI servers need more capacity, bandwidth and storage as models grow and inference contexts lengthen. Manufacturing technology, packaging, qualification and delivery reliability help differentiate supply, while customers can choose SK hynix and Samsung. Memory pricing still reflects supply, customer inventories and economic demand. More bits alone do not guarantee revenue or margins if selling prices fall faster than costs.

## What could break the thesis

The main threat is a supply and pricing reversal that outweighs bit growth, combined with customer inventory or financing stress. Contracts reduce that risk only within their enforceable scope; default or renegotiation can require litigation and cause losses even with deposits. Capacity can come online after demand peaks. Higher tax rates, grant clawbacks, export restrictions, yield problems or technology transitions can impair returns. Government incentives are conditional benefits, not unencumbered perpetual cash.

Credibility is **PASS** on reviewed evidence, including the auditor's unqualified financial/control opinions. A planned insider sale alone does not imply informed dumping.
