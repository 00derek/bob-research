### How the business creates value

Micron makes DRAM, high-bandwidth memory and NAND storage. AI servers need more capacity, bandwidth and storage as models grow and inference contexts lengthen. Manufacturing technology, packaging, qualification and delivery reliability help differentiate supply, while customers can choose SK hynix and Samsung.
