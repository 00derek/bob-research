## Investment thesis

Infineon's power products benefit from AI data-center construction, grid investment and richer electronics content in vehicles. The latest results show a recovery, but factories, acquisitions, compensation and senior capital claims consume part of that growth. The rebuilt assessment is **TRIM** at the October 30 close of **$56.35**, against **$45.77** probability-weighted present value, or −18.8% expected return. The strongest opposing case is that successful AI power ramps and higher utilization deliver the $118.62 bull outcome. The current price already requires a better outcome than the $41.75 base estimate. A conditional valuation reduction target follows from price exceeding 115% of weighted value, independently of weak technical timing.

## How the business creates value

Infineon makes power semiconductors, microcontrollers, sensors and connectivity/security products. Silicon, silicon carbide and gallium nitride address different voltage, efficiency and switching needs. Qualification, reliability, process expertise and breadth across a system can sustain design wins. These advantages compete with onsemi, STMicroelectronics and specialist suppliers. Large markets and more chips per device create an opportunity; shipment capacity, yields, customer demand and price determine the return earned on it.

The latest released period is fiscal Q3 ended June 30, reported [August 5](https://www.infineon.com/content/dam/infineon/row/public/documents/corporate/press/2026/infpr202608-125e.pdf). Fiscal year-end results are still future. EUR billions except per-unit data:
