## Business economics and competitive position

Infineon makes power semiconductors, microcontrollers, sensors and connectivity/security products. Silicon, silicon carbide and gallium nitride address different voltage, efficiency and switching needs. Qualification, reliability, process expertise and breadth across a system can sustain design wins. These advantages compete with onsemi, STMicroelectronics and specialist suppliers. Large markets and more chips per device create an opportunity; shipment capacity, yields, customer demand and price determine the return earned on it.

[October 27's Villach announcement](https://www.infineon.com/press-release/2026/infpr202610-021) adds material current evidence: €1.1 billion investment through 2034 for 300 mm GaN, with production scheduled by late 2027. The €319 million approved state aid still requires formal funding commitments and conditions. The model's sustained 12% capital intensity accommodates factory spending; it does not add the aid as current cash or attach an unsupported separate premium to the project. [October 21's NVIDIA collaboration](https://www.infineon.com/press-release/2026/infpr202610-012) supports relevance to 800 VDC power conversion, without establishing a quantified new profit stream.

Competition also has evidence behind it. [onsemi's August results](https://investor.onsemi.com/news-releases/news-release-details/onsemi-reports-second-quarter-2026-results) show a recovering power business and higher forward margins. Multiple suppliers are expanding capabilities. Infineon's breadth can win share, but a whole-industry recovery cannot be counted as exclusive company pricing power.

June cash/investments of €1.656 billion compare with €6.841 billion financial debt, €400 million lease liabilities and €166 million pension commitments. Hybrid capital carries €596 million but represents €600 million nominal nonordinary capital. The [annual report, pages 61/141](https://www.infineon.com/row/public/documents/corporate/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-v01-00-en.pdf), describes a perpetual instrument, discretionary distributions, a 3.625% coupon and first issuer call in January 2028. Investors cannot force repayment then. Reserving its claim protects ordinary valuation without inventing a mandatory 2028 refinancing crisis.

**Evidence gap:** Independent market-share, customer-level pricing, contract profitability and synchronized peer revisions are incomplete. The mechanism and competitive evidence support conditional scenarios, not a claim of monopoly pricing or a verified consensus error.
