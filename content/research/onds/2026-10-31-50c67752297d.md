## Investment thesis

Ondas is assembling autonomous defense, counter-drone and inspection businesses while orders expand quickly. The latest released quarter demonstrates revenue demand and a much larger backlog, but recurring cash losses and acquisition obligations remain the economic test. This new October 31 model resolves many share and settlement terms rather than relying on stale vendor share counts or warrant-driven net income. AVOID follows quality below the minimum floor and a weighted value below the completed close. The favorable alternative is that integrated products convert a growing order book into repeat deployments and operating leverage. The opposing case is that integration, dilution and sustained corporate costs consume the cash raised before common owners receive durable earnings.

## How the business creates value

Products span counter-UAS, precision strike, long-endurance surveillance, ground systems and inspection services. Customers buy systems, mission capability and support; different contract acceptance schedules make backlog conversion uneven. Reported June backlog was $613 million, or $757 million pro forma including DZYNE and Cyberhawk, which closed in Q3. Q2 orders were $175 million. October 7’s primary update adds more than $270 million orders since June, including $165 million from late August through October 1. Orders are evidence of adoption, not the whole addressable program or immediate revenue. The October 30 $250 million FEMA grant opportunity is not an Ondas award. [Current order update](https://www.ondas.com/post/ondas-secures-over-165-million-in-new-orders-since-last-order-update).

AeroVironment’s September 9 primary Q1 FY2027 release reports $480.5 million revenue, up 6%, $0.7 billion bookings and $1.5 billion funded backlog. It demonstrates an established scaled competitor, rather than proving equal products or Ondas market share. Domestic procurement and defense urgency support demand for multiple suppliers, but product qualification, manufacturing throughput and price/performance determine returns. [Current competitor result](https://investor.avinc.com/news-releases/news-release-details/aerovironment-announces-fiscal-2027-first-quarter-results).

**Evidence gap:** Current product-level market share, contract margins and complete peer earnings revisions are not all disclosed or independently comparable. Competitive observations support the mechanism, but do not establish monopoly pricing or the exact terminal growth assumed.
