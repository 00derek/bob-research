## Investment thesis

onsemi is recovering as automotive and industrial demand improves and AI power conversion adds content. The revised Synaptics acquisition changes the balance-sheet risk: the October cash agreement supersedes the earlier stock transaction. After rebuilding both the business forecast and the financing/claims bridge, the assessment remains **EXIT**. The October 30 close of $71.67 is above probability-weighted present value **$68.79**, offering−4.0% expected return, and the previously declared $76 closing setup has failed. No fresh qualified entry has replaced it. The strongest opposing case is that higher utilization, new power platforms and successful integration produce the $157.80 bull outcome. That possibility requires operating execution and does not repair the failed setup by itself.

## How the business creates value

onsemi designs power, analog and sensing semiconductors. Efficient conversion and control matter as vehicle voltages rise, industrial equipment electrifies and AI racks consume more power. Manufacturing process, packaging, reliability and customer qualification can protect design wins. These advantages compete with Infineon, STMicroelectronics and other power suppliers; customers can redesign, dual-source or delay purchases. Silicon carbide and gallium nitride are technology opportunities with capacity, yield and pricing risks, not automatic monopoly margins.
