## Investment thesis

onsemi sells the power and sensing devices that make vehicles, industrial equipment and data-center systems more efficient. Design wins and higher content can support growth, while factory utilization can amplify earnings in a recovery. That opportunity remains exposed to auto demand and competition in silicon carbide and other power technologies. At $71.67 on October 30, the completed close is below the declared $76 structural stop, and a compatible two-year standalone sensitivity gives a $70.37 weighted estimate, about 2% below price, before accounting for the pending Synaptics transaction. The assessment is **Exit** for the existing research setup. The strongest opposing case is a faster recovery in demand and margins, reinforced by AI power wins. That would need new reported evidence and a fresh qualified setup; a brief rebound above the old line does not erase the closing failure or justify widening risk to preserve a thesis.

## How the business creates value

Power conversion and sensing products become embedded in vehicle and industrial designs, creating customer qualification and switching costs. Silicon carbide can raise content in efficient electrification, while GaN and silicon solutions address adjacent requirements. Those technologies can also compete with each other. End-market demand, factory utilization and pricing determine whether design wins produce durable common earnings.

The [August 3 Q2 release](https://investor.onsemi.com/news-releases/news-release-details/onsemi-reports-second-quarter-2026-results) reports $1.604 billion revenue, up 9%, 38.4% GAAP gross margin and $0.56 GAAP diluted EPS, versus $0.74 adjusted. Free cash flow was $425.4 million and repurchases $332 million. Prior-quarter revenue was $1.513 billion. Returns exceeding sustainable cash, or capital-intensive capacity that does not earn its cost, would weaken the case; buybacks alone cannot establish value.

The [October 1 revised Synaptics agreement](https://investor.onsemi.com/news-releases/news-release-details/onsemi-and-synaptics-announce-revised-merger-agreement) is approximately $5.7 billion all-cash, funded with cash and committed debt, with expected completion by mid-2027 subject to approvals. Management expects adjusted EPS accretion, but that assertion is not a reconciled combined-company common-earnings forecast. **Evidence gap:** Financing cost, acquired cash/earnings, integration and purchase-price charges have not been bridged into these scenarios. The figures below are a standalone mathematical sensitivity, not a current transaction-adjusted intrinsic-value or entry/trim map.

The base FY2028 model requires $9 billion revenue, roughly 40% above annualized current sales, and 18% normalized common margin on400 million diluted units. It needs recovery and profitable content growth rather than simple extrapolation of adjusted quarterly EPS. **Evidence gap:** The full current capex/utilization, customer concentration, debt/cash and diluted-unit bridge is incomplete. Verify it with the upcoming results before any new entry review.
